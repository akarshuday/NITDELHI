# 🧬 MicrobeNet — AI-Powered Antibiotic Resistance Prediction

> **Predict. Detect. Fight Antibiotic Resistance.**

**MicrobeNet** is a genome-based antimicrobial resistance (AMR) prediction platform designed to identify potential antibiotic resistance directly from bacterial genome sequences.

Instead of waiting **24–72 hours** for traditional laboratory testing, the platform analyzes a bacterial **FASTA genome file**, detects known resistance genes, and generates an antibiotic resistance profile through a web-based dashboard.

---

## 🚨 The Problem

Antimicrobial resistance is one of the major challenges facing modern healthcare.

Bacteria are continuously developing resistance to antibiotics, making infections harder to treat.

Traditional laboratory-based resistance testing can take:

### ⏱️ 24–72 Hours

That delay can contribute to:

* ❌ Incorrect antibiotic treatment
* ⚠️ Increased risk to patients
* 🦠 Spread of resistant bacterial strains
* 🏥 Delayed clinical decision-making

### 💡 Our Goal

> **Can we use bacterial genomic information to predict antibiotic resistance faster?**

MicrobeNet explores this idea by analyzing bacterial genome sequences and identifying known antimicrobial resistance genes.

---

# 🔬 Our Solution

MicrobeNet follows a simple genomic analysis pipeline:

```text
        🧬 Bacterial Genome
              │
              ▼
       📄 FASTA Upload
              │
              ▼
      🔍 Gene Detection
              │
              ▼
       🗄️ AMR Database
              │
              ▼
     🧠 Resistance Prediction
              │
              ▼
       📊 Web Dashboard
              │
              ▼
       💾 Store Results

```

The user uploads a bacterial genome in **FASTA format**.

The system then:

1. Parses the genome sequence
2. Searches for known resistance genes
3. Matches detected genes against AMR data
4. Generates a resistance prediction
5. Displays the results through a web dashboard
6. Stores previous predictions in a database

# 🧠 How It Works

Imagine a bacterial genome as a **huge instruction book** 📖 containing millions of DNA characters.

Some parts of that book contain genetic information that can contribute to antibiotic resistance.

MicrobeNet looks for recognizable resistance-related genetic signatures.

```text
DNA Sequence
     ↓
AAAACTGCGT...
     ↓
Search for known AMR signatures
     ↓
mecA detected
     ↓
Methicillin resistance
     ↓
Display prediction

```

This turns complex genomic information into a more understandable resistance profile.
